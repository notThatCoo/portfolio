## Research

**DarkSide-20k neural-network surrogate**, UCR RISE 2025, with Alec Peck and Dr. Shawn Westerdale

- Built a neural-network surrogate that predicts light across 128 photodetector units of the Outer Veto in milliseconds, replacing Geant4 simulation that takes hours of CPU time per position.
- Reached 0.993 cosine similarity on held-out channel distributions using a two-head MLP (softmax for shape, Softplus for total light) trained on 1,463 filled voxels of a Geant4 light map.
- Coordinated the ML workstream for an inverse model that predicts 3D interaction position from the 128 channels; fixed overfitting with preprocessing changes and regularization, cutting validation loss from 0.292 to 0.074 and raising cosine similarity from 0.780 to 0.927.
- Paper available on request.
