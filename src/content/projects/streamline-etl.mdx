## Overview

A containerized ETL pipeline that pulls data from three kinds of sources (CSV files, APIs, and streams) and loads it into a PostgreSQL warehouse.

## Design

- **Queue:** **Redis Streams** between the sources and the warehouse.
- **Warehouse:** **PostgreSQL**.
- **Containers:** the stack runs with **Docker Compose**.
- **Idempotent loads:** rerunning a load doesn't duplicate data.
- **Tested:** 12 passing tests.
- **LLM helper:** writes SQL.
